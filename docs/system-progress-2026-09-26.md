## Research and mathematics boundaries

Hampton's work spans representation, quotient-state evolution, adaptive computation, coupling, memory, reasoning, learning, narrative/companion development, coordination, embodiment and controlled transitions. It should not be reduced to an output gate.

The 16 canonical state fields, archived 18-coordinate controller, five native pressures, domain-specific three-coordinate approach adapters and model/basis-specific latent assays are distinct structures. They are not one universal intelligence score. Inference, task learning, companion development and research/release must not count one event as four kinds of progress.

Some remaining work is unconnected existing code; some is installed behavior awaiting useful outcomes; some requires a new concrete implementation and consumer. The full R3 atlas/package has not been recovered locally. Bounded notes and reported equation counts cannot establish equation-by-equation completion; this does not block already-defined native work.

External papers provide mechanisms to compare/adapt, not proof of Hampton patent validity, priority or complete-stack efficacy. Hardware-only proposals, quantum perspectives and unrelated training systems remain attributed research until a compatible runtime and concrete consumer exist.

A single completion percentage would hide these differences. The status is **usable local development alpha; substantial native integration; incomplete learning/development breadth; failed reader qualification; unfinished public distribution and online participation**.
